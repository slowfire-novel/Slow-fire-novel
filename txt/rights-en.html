Who wrote it

These books were written by one author with an AI. The home page and the afterword to the Reader's Guide (Chinese) set out who did what. The share differs from work to work: more of the author's own is in 火德, in the stand-up edition of Humans Are More AI Than AI, and in 拉法叶赋. What the author claims is the author's own creative work: the text written by hand, and the selection, arrangement and revision of the whole.
