火德·乐

Everything written for 《火德》 in one place: six pieces for the four directions, the motifs everything else is built from, and a short song for each main character's moment. Unless a slip says otherwise, every pitch is a whole-number ratio over Hefei's 50 Hz grid.

人物 life songs

One minute each, built the way a film cue is built: a memory, the motif, the build, the moment, the motif alone. Each is cut from the motifs below and coloured by the character's historical prototype.

动机 motifs

Each motif is one idea, short enough to recognise wherever it turns up. The life songs are made of them.

四方 the four directions

The last line of 大风 asks for brave men to guard the four quarters. These pieces go to the middle and to the four edges, each tied to a chapter. Tunes are new, in the manner of each tradition.

Made in code tonight: every sound is built from sine waves and filtered noise. Each strip shows how loud the piece is over its length, and you can click it to jump.

"In the manner of" means exactly that. The tunes are new; the instruments, modes and rhythms are modelled on the traditions they name, by someone who cannot hear the result. Checked by measurement, not by ear.
