火德·乐

Everything written for 《火德》 in one place: six pieces for the four directions, the motifs everything else is built from, and a short song for each main character's moment. Unless a slip says otherwise, every pitch is a whole-number ratio over Hefei's 50 Hz grid.

人物 life songs

One minute each, built the way a film cue is built: a memory, the motif, the build, the moment, the motif alone. Each is cut from the motifs below and coloured by the character's historical prototype.

动机 motifs

Each motif is one idea, short enough to recognise wherever it turns up. The life songs are made of them.

四方 the four directions

The last line of 大风 asks for brave men to guard the four quarters. These pieces go to the middle and to the four edges, each tied to a chapter. Tunes are new, in the manner of each tradition.

Composed in code for 《火德》, a side volume of 《阴燃 · The Slow Fire》. The tuning comes from the book's two power grids, Hefei's 50 Hz and Lafayette's 60 Hz, a minor third apart; most pieces stand on one of them, and some bells carry both. Where a free recording exists, the instrument is a real one, re-pitched note by note to these tunings: erhu, guzheng, cello, guitars, drum kits, bass, ocarina, frame drums, horns and piano. The voices, suona, xiao, leaf, guqin, pipa and throat singing are still built from sound itself, because no free recording of them could be found. Each strip shows how loud the piece is over its length; click it to jump.

"In the manner of" means exactly that. The tunes are new; the instruments, modes and rhythms follow the traditions they name. They were made by a composer who cannot hear: every change was measured first, and the author's ears had the last word.
